# H16 -- the NYC persistent-cloud regime is not in the price

WRITTEN 2026-09-14, BEFORE ANY TEST. The stratum, the bar, the baselines and
the retirement rule are fixed here and DO NOT MOVE. Corrections are appended
with a reason, like every other correction in this repository.

## THE CLAIM IS ABOUT THE MARKET, NOT THE WEATHER

Stated in H15's words because the distinction is the same one:

    CLAIM: on NO_BURN days at KNYC the market's implied distribution is
    MIS-SHAPED -- centred on the blend of cloudy and clear outcomes rather than
    on the regime, which is observable from that morning's own sky groups.

    PREDICTION: market Brier on NO_BURN days is WORSE than on NO_STRATUS days
    by >= 0.02, AND a regime-conditioned model beats the market on that subset.

A tie, a reversal, or the market scoring equally well on both strata falsifies
it. It does not license a variant: no re-cutting the strata afterwards, no
restricting to months discovered later. Those are new registrations.

## THE STRATUM IS `NO_BURN` ALONE, AND THAT IS NOT H15's STRATUM

H15 tests LATE_BURN + NO_BURN together, because at LAX both suppress. **At NYC
they do not**, and the difference is a mechanism, not a tuning choice. Deviation
from each day's own month mean, `city_regimes.json`, n=1,714 days 2022-2026:

| regime | full year | Aug 15 - Oct 5 |
|---|---|---|
| NO_STRATUS | +0.31 (n=1216) | +1.25 (n=153) |
| EARLY_BURN | **+2.64** (n=101) | **+1.66** (n=25) |
| LATE_BURN | +0.71 (n=119) | -0.29 (n=20) |
| NO_BURN | **-2.64** (n=278) | **-5.67** (n=40) |

At NYC morning cloud that BURNS OFF runs *warmer* than a clear day -- it rides a
moist, warm-advected airmass. Only cloud that PERSISTS suppresses the high. So
LATE_BURN at NYC (-0.29F in the window) is indistinguishable from NO_STRATUS,
and pooling it in the way H15 pools it at LAX would dilute the test stratum with
a cell carrying no signal. The stratum is NO_BURN. This is decided HERE, on
weather data, before any price is scored.

H15 could say `logs/poll_lax/` held 1 row when it was written. H16 cannot say
anything like it, and the difference is recorded rather than glossed:

  poll rows each), of which **two are NO_BURN** (2026-09-02, 2026-09-13).
* **The NYC market is already measured.** CANDIDATE.md's 2026-09-12 price
  comparison reports NYC Brier 0.0593 for the market against 0.1547 for the
  model -- the market 2.6x better. This registration is made knowing that.

What can honestly be claimed: `city_regime.py` was frozen 2026-09-14 from free
weather archives, its windows and 3000 ft threshold COPIED from LAX's frozen
definition rather than fitted, and no NYC quote was read while building it. The
poll logs were opened to count rows, not to read prices.

What follows from the disclosure: **the two existing NO_BURN days do not count
toward the bar.** Units are forward-only from 2026-09-14. They are recorded
separately as a pre-registration cohort and may be reported, never pooled.

## THIS DOES NOT REPORT BEFORE THE STOP. SAYING SO IS THE POINT.

NYC NO_BURN share by month over 2022-2026 is 8-22%, pooled **Sep-Dec 17.4%**.
At ~1 NYC day per calendar day, 20 forward NO_BURN days needs **~115 calendar
days**, reaching the bar around **2027-01-07**.

**The programme stops 2026-12-31.** On the measured rate H16 arrives at roughly
**18 of 20 units** by then.

H15.md drew exactly this line -- "This is EXPECTED TO REPORT, unlike H8b which
was registered knowing it could not" -- and H16 falls on the H8b side. It is
registered anyway because CANDIDATE.md forbids adding any avenue after
2026-10-31, so registering now preserves an option that expires, while shrinking
the bar to fit the calendar would be the bar-shopping H15 refused. The bar stays
at 20.

Three ways this resolves, and the choice is the operator's, not this file's:

1. Accept an under-powered readout at ~18 units and report it AS under-powered.
2. Move the stop. CANDIDATE.md permits this explicitly and only now -- "Change
   this date now if it is wrong. It cannot be changed once passed."
3. Let it expire unreported, which is a real outcome and costs nothing but the
   collection.

**No unit is lost by deciding later.** IEM's hourly archive is permanent, so any
past day's regime is re-derivable, and `logs/poll/` is already capturing the NYC
book daily. The harness can be built at any time without losing a single day.

## BASELINES, BOTH NAMED NOW

  B1 -- the market's implied distribution from the bottom-rung book at the same
        instant, the object `edge_test.py` already scores.
  B2 -- the same regime-conditioned model scored on NYC NO_STRATUS days.

H16 passes only if the market is WORSE on NO_BURN days than on clear ones AND
the conditioned model beats it there. Beating B2 alone is meaningless: it would
only say cloudy days are easier to forecast, which the sd column already hints.

## UNITS AND THE BAR

  Unit: one (day, city=NYC) with a settled CLI high, a NO_BURN assignment from
        `city_regime.regime()`, and >= 20 poll rows carrying the bottom-rung
        book. Forward-only from 2026-09-14.

  Bar:  20 DISTINCT NO_BURN days, matching H15's, H4b's and H6's bar rather
        than a fresh one chosen after seeing how fast they accrue.

## WHY THE SAMPLE CANNOT BE INFLATED

Not by polling more often -- the unit is a day. Not by pooling NO_STRATUS in,
which is the control stratum. Not by adding LATE_BURN, which the table above
shows carries no signal at NYC and whose inclusion would be discovered
convenient rather than argued. Not by adding EWR, PHL, DC, BOS or TTN: they
share the sign pattern and the synoptic systems, so five northeast cities on one
day is close to ONE independent observation, and pooling them would inflate n
without inflating information. That is a separate registration needing an
explicit correlation treatment.

## NO PILOT

H16 cannot promote anything. It is an information claim about a price. A
trading claim would be a separate registration with its own 60 units, its own
Wilson LCB bar and its own kill lines.

## RETIREMENT

Per the futility convention: H16 retires when the Wilson 95% UPPER bound on
"market Brier worse on NO_BURN days" falls below the no-difference point. Same
interval as promotion, other end. A retired H16 is refuted at 95% and does not
come back by adding days.
