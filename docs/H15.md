# H15 -- the LAX marine-layer regime is not in the price

WRITTEN 2026-09-12. The classifier below was built, measured and FROZEN on this
date from free weather data with **no price data in the loop** -- there is none
to have: `logs/poll_lax/` was created the same day and holds 1 row. Nothing here
can have been tuned against a market, and that separation is the whole point.
H4a's instrument was frozen before scoring and still failed honestly; one tuned
after seeing prices would tell us nothing either way.

## THE CLAIM IS ABOUT THE MARKET, NOT THE WEATHER

The weather leg is already measured and is NOT the hypothesis. Stated plainly so
it cannot later be presented as the finding:

    CLAIM: on marine-layer days at KLAX the market's implied distribution is
    MIS-SHAPED -- too wide, or centred on the blend of burn and no-burn
    outcomes rather than on the regime that is already observable by 10:00 PDT.

    PREDICTION: market Brier on LATE_BURN + NO_BURN days is WORSE than on
    NO_STRATUS days by >= 0.02, and a regime-conditioned model beats the market
    on that subset.

A tie, a reversal, or the market scoring equally well on both strata falsifies
it. It does not license a variant -- no re-cutting the regimes after the fact,
no restricting to a subset of months discovered later. Those are new
registrations with their own units.

## WHY THIS IS NOT H3 AGAIN

H3 (settlement gap, NOT SUPPORTED -- "real but priced") and the 2026-09-12 price
comparison (market Brier 0.0298 vs model 0.0948, 3.2x) both say the same thing:
a real weather signal can be entirely inside the price. That is the DEFAULT
expectation here, and H15 is written expecting to fail.

What makes it worth registering at all is that both of those were measured at
KNYC and KDEN -- continental and plains stations where the market was measurably
excellent. Neither says anything about a coastal boundary regime. The claim is
narrow and the prior is poor. If it fails, the record simply gains a third
instance of "real but priced," which is itself worth having.

## THE CLASSIFIER -- FROZEN, FREE DATA, MORNING-ONLY

Decided from KLAX hourly sky groups alone, all knowable by 17Z (10:00 PDT),
before the peak. "Stratus" = a BKN or OVC layer below 3000 ft.

    NO_STRATUS   no stratus in 12Z-18Z
    EARLY_BURN   stratus at 12Z-14Z, gone by 16Z-18Z
    LATE_BURN    stratus still at 16Z-18Z, gone by 19Z-21Z
    NO_BURN      stratus still at 19Z-21Z (1pm PDT)

Implementation: `<scratchpad>/hfasos/laxregime.py`, function `regime()`.
Thresholds (3000 ft, the three hour-windows) are set here and DO NOT MOVE. If
they ever need to, the change is recorded with a reason, like every other
correction in this file.

## THE WEATHER LEG, ALREADY MEASURED (context, not the hypothesis)

n=1713 LAX days with a CLI high and full hourly sky, 2022-2026.

Pooled WITHIN-MONTH deviation from that month's own mean -- month removed, so
this is not a seasonal proxy:

| regime | days | vs month mean |
|---|---|---|
| NO_STRATUS | 900 | **+2.00 F** |
| EARLY_BURN | 258 | -0.83 F |
| LATE_BURN | 283 | -1.83 F |
| NO_BURN | 255 | **-4.01 F** |

Trading window (Aug 15 - Oct 5), n=236, season mean 78.1:

| regime | n | mean high | vs season | sd |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NO_STRATUS | 123 | 81.2 | +3.1 | 6.41 |
| EARLY_BURN | 43 | 76.4 | -1.6 | 2.80 |
| LATE_BURN | 44 | 74.9 | -3.2 | **2.67** |
| NO_BURN | 26 | 71.7 | -6.4 | **2.96** |

The variance collapse matters more than the mean shift: pooled seasonal sd is
7.05, and conditioning on a burn regime cuts it to 2.7-3.0. September alone
spans NO_STRATUS 82.0 to NO_BURN 71.2 -- eleven degrees inside one month.

**None of this is an edge.** It is the reason the market claim is worth asking.

## BASELINES, BOTH NAMED NOW

  B1 -- the market's implied distribution from the bottom-rung book at the same
        instant, the same object `edge_test.py` already scores.
  B2 -- the same regime-conditioned model scored on NO_STRATUS days.

H15 passes only if the market is WORSE on marine strata than on clear ones AND
our conditioned model beats it there. Beating B2 alone is meaningless: it would
only say marine days are easier to forecast, which the sd column already shows.

## UNITS AND THE BAR

  Unit: one (day, city=LAX) with a settled CLI high, a regime assignment, and
        >= 20 poll rows carrying the bottom-rung book.

  Bar:  20 DISTINCT DAYS in the marine strata (LATE_BURN + NO_BURN combined),
        matching H4b's and H6's bar rather than a fresh one chosen after
        seeing how fast they accrue.

  RATE, computed now so it is not discovered in November. Marine strata share
  by month over 2022-2026: Sep 32%, Oct 34%, Nov 20%, Dec 28%; pooled Sep-Dec
  **29%**. At ~1 LAX day per calendar day, 20 marine days needs ~**70 calendar
  days**. Poll coverage began 2026-09-12, so the bar is reached around
  **2026-11-20**, leaving 41 days before the 2026-12-31 stop.

  This is EXPECTED TO REPORT, unlike H8b which was registered knowing it could
  not. The margin is real but not large: a fortnight of lost poll coverage, or a
  dry autumn running below the 29% marine rate, would push it past the stop.

## WHY THE SAMPLE CANNOT BE INFLATED

Not by polling more often -- the unit is a day. Not by pooling NO_STRATUS in --
that is the control stratum and pooling destroys the comparison. Not by adding
SAN or SFO, which are different coastal regimes and would be a new
registration. Not by relaxing the 3000 ft threshold after seeing results.

## NO PILOT

H15 cannot promote anything. It is an information claim about a price, the same
status H4a's 1.48F held before its held-out test -- and H4a then failed at
-6.16%. A trading claim on this would be a separate registration with its own
60 units, its own Wilson LCB bar and its own kill lines.

## RETIREMENT

Per the futility convention: H15 retires when the Wilson 95% UPPER bound on
"market Brier worse on marine strata" falls below the no-difference point. Same
interval as promotion, other end. A retired H15 is refuted at 95% and does not
come back by adding days.
